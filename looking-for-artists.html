Looking for artists

The Seed by VZero911 is a Web3 + AI monster-collecting game. The chain, the server and the game exist; it needs faces worth the world behind it: dark magic, a floating island, a giant scary tower.

Who

  • 3D artists: monsters, props, the Village island and its tower (glTF, low to mid poly, for three.js and mobile).
  • 2D artists and illustrators: monster art and skins, rune and item icons, banners, key art.
  • UI artists: game screens on the V2 design tokens (gold frames, display font).
  • Sound and music: not yet, later.

What you get

Credit, and a share of the project's contribution points (POC: earned, never bought). Real money only after audits and a legal frame.

How

See what exists in the showcase, then write to vzero911@gmail.com or on Discord (vzero911).
